What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyberattack in which criminals use deceptive messages, websites or links to trick people into revealing sensitive information or performing unsafe actions.

Common Types of Phishing

Email Phishing

Fraudulent emails designed to deceive recipients into clicking harmful links or sharing sensitive information.

Spear Phishing

Targeted phishing messages designed for a particular individual or organisation.

Smishing

Phishing attacks carried out through SMS or text messages.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Verify website addresses before clicking.
  • Be careful with unexpected attachments.
  • Never share passwords through suspicious links.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Report suspicious emails and messages.
